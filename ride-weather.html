      Looking ahead

      Monday to Friday, this week and next, skipping days already past. Temperatures are at 8 AM and 4 PM. Rain chance is the highest from 8 AM to 4 PM. Snow is the total for the whole day. Forecasts get less reliable further out, and Open-Meteo may leave the rain chance blank (n/a) for the furthest days.

      Your cutoffs

      Temperatures are feels-like in °C. The page uses the coldest hour from 8 AM to 4 PM, rounded to a whole degree. Each number below is a switch point between two neighbouring pieces: the warmer piece is worn at or above it and the colder piece below it, so changing one number moves the edge of both.
